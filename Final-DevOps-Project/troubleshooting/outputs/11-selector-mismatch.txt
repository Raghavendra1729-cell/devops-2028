### Break it: point the Service at a label no Pod has
$ kubectl -n troubleshooting patch service notes -p '{"spec":{"selector":{"app":"notes-wrong"}}}'

### Root cause: selector app=notes-wrong matches no Pod, so the Service has no endpoints
### Fix: put the selector back to app=notes
$ kubectl -n troubleshooting patch service notes -p '{"spec":{"selector":{"app":"notes"}}}'
service/notes patched
